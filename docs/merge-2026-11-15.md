## F. Obsolete systems (removed or reworked)

- **Automatic discovery.**
  - `Forensics.updateDiscovery` revealed a wreck within `wreckVisibleWithoutDroneM` (160 m) and Ingenuity revealed all wrecks.
  - New rule: a wreck exists for the player only after it is uncovered by digging or breaking ice (`s.forensics[id].uncovered`). Until then it is not drawn and not on the strip, and the goal card does not name it.
  - Ingenuity keeps a real but smaller effect: it scouts sand pits, as in local-wip. RIMFAX, the ground-penetrating radar, marks which mound or ice hides something.
- **Goal `wreck_signal` with a distance** → becomes "explore: east dig field / west glacier", with no position.
- **Text-only EDL panel:** the landing now plays in the world (ana). Its keys are left unused.
- **Old 15 s timed transit:** already replaced by the task chain; ana's canvas becomes its backdrop.
- **sude's `evaGameMinutesPerRealSecond` and walk speed 90 m/min:** phase 1 (`116aa65`) already slowed the clock to 2 game min/s for all scenes, and ana derives the on-screen walk from speed × clock. Taking 90 would double the walk. Kept: 45 m/min, one clock.
- **medine's runtime patching** (overriding `FX.render`, `Surface.update`, `Game.interact`, `playTransit`): its mechanics are ported into the real modules. The prototype file is not loaded.
- **nursedef's `.png → .jpeg` renames:** not taken, because the content is PNG and every loader uses `.png`.

## G. Final architecture

- **Map** (`terrain.map`, metres, airlock = 0, one scale outside the base):
  - **West, glacier**, from −120 to −5,350 m, ice wall at −5,000 m. Built on sude's `westRegion`, with ana's ice clusters moved onto the ice field.
  - **Vehicles, west:** Spirit (furrow → sand-and-frost dig), Phoenix (heat shield and chute → break the CO₂ ice), Beagle 2 (bounce marks → dig under the overhang), the fictional cargo lander (ice cave) and the DS2 probe (ice wall).
  - **Base**, from −120 to +100 m.
  - **East, lava plain**, from +100 to +1,500 m, in four zones:
    - lake bed with Opportunity's tracks → dust mound;
    - dunes with the sand pit → InSight dust mound;
    - crater field with boulders;
    - lava plain with tumuli and collapse pits → rubble-sealed skylight at +1,400 m.
  - **Vehicles, east:** Opportunity, InSight and the fictional SCOUT-1 inside the tube.
  - All five real cases are now on the map; `activeCaseIds` lists all five.
- **Dig sites** (`data/explore_config.json` `digSites`): `{id, atM, kind: dust|sand|ice|rubble|overhang, hits, minutesPerHit, reveals: caseId|tube|part|null}`.
  - One `S.Explore.dig(s, id)` handles them all.
  - Each hit costs game time, so suit O₂, battery and cold keep running.
  - sude's barriers, ice cave and probe keep their own data and use the same hit model.
- **Wreck flow:** uncover → (existing) evidence minigames with FIELD CAM for photo → diagnosis → report → salvage.
- **Goals** come from what the player has done (uncovered, evidence, tube opened), not from where they stand.
- **Opening** (`cutscenes.js` + `opening.js`):
  - The canvas time runs freely through liftoff.
  - During the cruise it follows the task progress.
  - After the last task it runs freely through the approach, then hands over to `S.Lander` in the world.
- **Labelling:** every new position, hit count and minute value is a game value (`simplified: true` with a note). Real trail details (Spirit's wheel, Phoenix's chute and heat shield, Beagle 2's airbags, DS2, THEMIS skylight temperatures) keep their NASA sources. SCOUT-1, the cargo lander and the lava tube are labelled fictional.
