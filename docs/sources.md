# Sources — Wreck cases (K5)

All wreck-case text in `content/story.json` (`wrecks.*`) is checked against the sources below.
Numbers used in the game are proposed for `nasa_data.json` with a `src` field (see `nasa_data.wrecks.json`).

## 1. Opportunity (NASA, MER-B)
- NASA mission page: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mer-opportunity/
- JPL — mission end (13 Feb 2019): https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasas-opportunity-rover-mission-comes-to-end/
- JPL — recovery efforts, last tau 10.8 vs. typical 0.5: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/six-things-about-opportunitys-recovery-efforts/
- JPL — status reports, storm first detected 30 May 2018: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/opportunity-hunkers-down-during-dust-storm/
- NASA — "On Mars, Light Is Energy" (tau vs. watt-hours graphic): https://science.nasa.gov/resource/on-mars-light-is-energy/
- NASA Photojournal PIA22930 — final-day Pancam tau: https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA22930
- The Planetary Society — ~645 Wh on 1 June, ~22 Wh on 10 June: https://www.planetary.org/articles/06-mer-update-opportunity-dust-storm-sleep

> Note: NASA sources give the last energy value as 21 Wh (graphic) or 22 Wh (team statements). The game uses "about 21–22 Wh".

> Correction applied: agency attribution is ESA / UK. NASA's role is the MRO HiRISE images that found the lander, so the in-game card is labelled "Finding: ESA / United Kingdom", not "NASA finding".

## Design note
- The task card's "black box" evidence is shown in-game as **"Last telemetry"**. Mars landers have no flight recorders; their last downlinked data is the real equivalent. Beagle 2 has none, so its card says "No record".
- Inner-voice lines are fiction written for the game. They contain no facts beyond those in the finding card.
